Moved the 28 EPUB classes out of `engineering_influence.publishing.epub` into the layers: `models/epub` (`EpubContents`, `XhtmlPatterns`, and a new `CoverDeclaration` for the cover's file name, id and size floor), `services/epub` (the document builders, the checks, `EpubBuilder`, `EpubVerifier` and `EpubRepacker`) and `infrastructure/epub` (`EpubArchive`, `CoverImage`, `EpubcheckRunner`, `Tex4ebookCompiler`, plus the new `EpubCoverInspector` and `ZipEpubPackage`). The services no longer open archives or images themselves: they use new `EpubCompilerInterface`, `EpubArchiveReaderInterface`, `EpubcheckInterface`, `EpubCoverInspectorInterface`, `EpubPackageInterface` and `EbookCoverInterface` ports, and `BuildWorkspaceInterface` gained `prepare_ebook` and `ensure_directory`. `EpubBuilder`, `EpubVerifier` and `EpubRepacker` take their collaborators as arguments, `PublishingController` takes a finished `EpubBuilder` and `EpubVerifier` instead of building them, and `PublishingControllerFactory` wires the real ones, so the controller no longer imports an adapter. `CoverImage.NAME`, `ID` and `MIN_PX` are now on `CoverDeclaration`. The EPUBs `ei book epub` and `ei book publish` write, and the `ei` commands and their output, are unchanged.
