A revised shooting script can now mark its changed scenes with their revision colour. `book create --previous-script FILE --revision N` gives the earlier run's `shooting_script.fountain` as the baseline; revision 1 is Blue, 2 is Pink, and so on through the standard order up to 9. Each scene is matched to its baseline scene by its number, so a scene keeps its identity when others are added or cut. A new or changed scene gets a `[[revised: Colour *]]` note above its heading, and the marked script is stored as `revision_pages.fountain`. The shooting script itself is unchanged. Compatible: both flags are optional, and a run without a baseline stores no revision pages.
