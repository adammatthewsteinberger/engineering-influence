Moved object composition out of the interactions and into `runtime/`. `ApiApplication(controller, guard, observability)` and `McpApplication(controller)` now take a project controller instead of a ledger and queue, `CliApplication` and `ServeCli` receive their collaborators, and `VibeyPhaseHandler` takes a `RunPhaseInterface`. The `ei` entry point is now `engineering_influence.runtime.main:main`, the CLI application class moved to `interactions.cli.cli_application`, and webhook delivery and signing moved from `interactions.webhooks` to `infrastructure.webhooks` (a new `WebhookSignerInterface` lets the inbound verifier stay in the interactions). `lint-imports` now enforces a strict layers contract. The `ei` command, the REST API, the MCP tool and the SDK are unchanged.
