`ei evaluate` is the A5 and A6 evaluation harness. It makes every book of a fixed set (`evaluation/set.toml`: three non-fiction ideas and two novels) with the stack's own pipeline, has a separately configured judge model score each finished book on a reviewed rubric (`evaluation/rubric.toml`), and checks the release against the bar: every book finished, every dimension's mean at least 3.5, none fallen by more than 0.2 from the previous report, and the cost estimate within 30% of what was spent. It writes a stable `evaluation.json` that is the next release's baseline, and exits 0, 1 or 2. It spends real money, so it refuses to start without `EI_ALLOW_PAID=1`, takes a budget for each book and one for the judge, and the judge reserves every call from its own budget before it is made. Tested only against scripted models: no real model has been run against the bar. A nightly workflow exists and is off until `RUN_NIGHTLY_EVAL` is `true`. See [EVALUATION.md](docs/EVALUATION.md).
