A screenplay run now audits its source before it plans the film, and sets the look of the film before it drafts. The new `audit` stage writes a verdict on every source file (in order and numbered from 001), how each major character is internalized, the source each beat comes from, what the film compresses, and what it adds; every addition must be marked FILM. The new `visual_grammar` stage writes the light, colour and texture of each primary set and a visual signature for each major character. Both are checked before they are stored. The anchors and beats stages now read the source files under their numbers rather than the brief's inputs. Compatible: the anchors and beats prompts move to version 2, two new prompts ship, and runs already past the beat stage keep the answers they stored.
