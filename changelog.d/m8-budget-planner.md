A run now plans its money. **The editing passes degrade instead of stopping the run**: when a revision pass would pass the cap, that chapter and the rest of the pass keep the text they had (stored unchanged, so the next pass and the assembly read them as usual), the pass's list says so, and one tolerated issue, `revision-skipped-budget`, goes into the quality report; nothing more is paid for. Where the pass stopped is kept in `<stage>_skipped.md`, so a redelivery reports the same list. The planning stages and the draft are still essential and still stop to ask for more budget. **A first-pass cost estimator** (`CostEstimator`) prices every stage of a plan from the brief (words become chapters, chapters become the tokens each stage reads and writes) with the model's own rates, and a `BudgetPlanner` grades a cap against it: `short`, `tight`, `lean` or `ample`. `ei book create` prints the estimate before it spends anything, refuses a `short` budget with the budget that would do (`--ignore-estimate` starts anyway), and warns on `tight` and `lean`. **`quality-report.json` gains a `spend` block** with the cap, what was spent, the estimate made from the brief and their ratio, which is how the estimator's constants are to be calibrated on real runs: nothing here claims the estimate is within any bound of the truth, and A5's accuracy target is not yet evidenced. A test shrinks a finished draft's cap to what it spent and shows the run still completes with every chapter assembled from the draft.
