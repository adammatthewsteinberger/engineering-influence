Moved the 21 typesetting classes out of `engineering_influence.publishing.typesetting` into the layers: `errors/typesetting` (`BookBuildError`, `ManuscriptError`), `models/typesetting`, `services/typesetting` (the Markdown-to-LaTeX converters and `BookBuilder`), `repositories/typesetting` (`BookLoader`, `ChapterLoader`) and `infrastructure/typesetting` (`LatexCompiler`, `ToolEnvironment`). `PathRelativizer` and `RunningHead`, which the loaders share, are now models. `BookBuilder` takes its loader, compiler, workspace and assets as `BookLoaderInterface`, `LatexCompilerInterface`, `BuildWorkspaceInterface` and `PackageAssetsInterface` arguments, and `PublishingController` takes them too; a new `PublishingControllerFactory` in `runtime/` builds them for `ei book`, so no layer below `runtime` imports an adapter. `PackageAssets(override)` can serve a directory of your own. The `ei` commands and their output are unchanged.
