Added the model catalog: `ModelCatalog` picks the price in force on a date for a provider and model, and `TomlModelCatalogLoader` reads it from a TOML file the deployment owns (`docs/MODEL_CATALOG.md`). Prices are integer micro-dollars per million tokens, each with an effective date, so old calls keep their old price and a price change is a new entry. A model with no price in force is refused (`MODEL_UNPRICED`) rather than guessed, and the package ships no vendor prices. `ProviderDescriptor` now prices a long-context tier (a prompt over a threshold is billed at a higher percentage of the input and output rates), in both the final cost and the pre-call reservation.
