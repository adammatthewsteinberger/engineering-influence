A non-fiction run now has a `draft` stage after `outline`. It writes every chapter in order (`chapter_01.md`, `chapter_02.md`, …), each from the brief, the positioning, the outline and research, the summaries of the chapters before it and the end of the last one, and keeps a short summary of each (`summary_01.md`, …) and an index (`draft.md`). A stage that fails or is delivered twice goes back over the chapters and does only the work that is not done, so a redelivery costs nothing for what was already written. Chapter length comes from the brief's target words (2,500 words a chapter when there is none), within the model's output limit and window; a window that cannot hold a chapter stops the stage before any call. Skills can be enabled for `draft` like any other stage. Recorded in ADR 0010; nothing here has run against a live model yet.
