The structure and lines editing passes now tell the model how long the chapter is and what range to stay in, and a rejected answer is asked for once more. On the first real book, `gpt-oss:20b` shrank every chapter to 21-46% of its length in the structure pass and 57-80% in the lines pass, so all but one answer fell outside the bounds and the book was kept as drafted. Asking for "about as long, no fewer than N words and no more than M, keeping every section, table, checklist and example" brought six of the seven chapters to 90-100% of their length, with every heading and table kept and no new quotations; the seventh, at 62%, is the case the retry below is for. If an answer is still outside the bounds, the executor asks once more with the rejected answer's length and the range in the prompt, which makes it a different request and so not a replay from the journal; if that one does not finish, the first answer's rejection stands, and if it runs out of budget the chapter is skipped like any other. The three editing templates are now version 2 (each has a new `retry` part; the structure and lines prompts have the three length blanks), so an old run's calls are not replayed. A rejected chapter now costs a second call, which the cost estimator does not count.
