Screenplay runs now render their assembled script to a PDF. After assembly, a `render_script` stage lays the Fountain out in standard screenplay pages (US Letter, 12 point Courier, the usual indents for character, dialogue, parenthetical and transitions), typesets it with LuaLaTeX, and stores `screenplay.pdf` with a `render.md` giving its page count. A script that does not typeset stops the run with the end of LaTeX's log in the message. A film over the 195-page ceiling stops the run; a film past three hours (180 pages) is recorded as a note and the run finishes. Compatible: the screenplay plan has one more stage after `assemble_script`, the cost estimate treats rendering as having no model call, and no existing run is affected. The run's issue codes gain two new entries, `revision-kept-draft` and `screenplay-length`; adding codes is compatible, and existing codes are unchanged.
