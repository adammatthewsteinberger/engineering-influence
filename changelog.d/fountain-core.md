Groundwork for automated screenplays: the pure Fountain core, with no model call and no new run kind yet. The parser reads a script's title page, its elements and its scenes (scene headings with their `[[key: value]]` notes). The format check applies prompt 09's mechanical test: FADE IN: and FADE OUT. then THE END, allowed times of day, no camera directions except INSERT, no digits in dialogue, parentheticals that lead into dialogue and stay under a tenth of speeches, and no year in a heading in place of (FLASHBACK). Interior thought in action is reported as a note. A Courier page estimate and a Fountain answer screen (refusals are read from action, not dialogue) build on the parser. Compatible: no existing behaviour changes.
