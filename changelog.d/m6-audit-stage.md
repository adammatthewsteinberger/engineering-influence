A non-fiction run now ends with an `audit` stage that runs the publishing pipeline's own audit-only gates (the manuscript audit and the release readiness check) on the assembled book, with no model call and no TeX toolchain. The reports are stored under `audit/` with a summary in `audit.md`. Errors stop the run with `AUDIT_FAILED`, after the reports are stored so a failed book can be read; warnings, such as a missing price, do not. The book is laid out in a private temporary folder that is removed afterwards. An assembled book passes the real audit (with an author profile the only findings are the prices the author has yet to set). The PDF and EPUB build and the cover are not part of it yet. Recorded in ADR 0010.
