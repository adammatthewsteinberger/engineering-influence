Screenplay runs now revise each drafted scene and then assemble the script. After drafting, a `revise_scenes` stage asks the model once per scene for a tighter revision, and keeps it only if it passes the Fountain scene check and stays within 10 per cent of the draft's length; otherwise the drafted scene is kept, and the report says how many scenes kept their draft. An `assemble_script` stage then joins the revised scenes, in card order, into `screenplay.fountain`, and refuses a scene that is not exactly one scene, or a script that breaks the whole-script format rules. Drafting no longer writes the script. Compatible: the new `screenplay_revise_scene` prompt is version 1, the screenplay plan has two more stages after `draft`, and the cost estimate prices the revision as one call per scene and leaves it out when the money runs out.
