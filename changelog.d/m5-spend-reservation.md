Added `CallModel`, the one way a stage reaches a model, and `SpendGuard`, the lock-protected budget it spends against. A request already answered in the run and stage is replayed from the journal for nothing. Otherwise the worst-case cost is reserved before anything is sent, so concurrent calls can never pass the cap, the call is made, and the answer is journaled with its true cost before the reservation is settled. A failed call releases its reservation and journals nothing. A truncated or refused answer is journaled too, so its cost counts toward spend, but under a key that includes the outcome and the attempt, so it is never replayed as a good answer. A call that costs more than it reserved is journaled and then refused with `BudgetExceededError`. Nothing calls it yet.
