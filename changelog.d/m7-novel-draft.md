A novel run now drafts (M7.4): its plan ends with `draft`, the same chapter-by-chapter loop non-fiction uses, driven by a new `DraftRecipe` that says what a kind of book reads (the brief, the story bible, the beats and the outline), how the prompt labels it, and the one instruction, which now also carries the brief's violence and heat levels. Two new prompts, `novel_context_pack` and `novel_summary`; the non-fiction prompts are byte-for-byte what they were. When every chapter is written, `NovelDraftChecks` reads the whole draft against the story bible with the continuity checker and records each finding as a non-blocking quality issue (`CONTINUITY_NAME`, `_PLACE`, `_AGE`, `_TIMELINE`) and in `continuity.md`; the run keeps going, because a finding is a question and not a verdict. The novel draft is also gated by the content-level check before any call. Revision, copy editing, assembly and the audit for a novel are not built, nothing repairs a flagged chapter, and nothing has run against a real model. See `docs/NOVELS.md`.
