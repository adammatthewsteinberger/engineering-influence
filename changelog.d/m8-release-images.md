A release now publishes the two container images (A9). After the GitHub release, an `images` job in `release.yml` builds the `runtime` and `worker-tex` targets of the `Dockerfile` from the released commit, checks that each reports the released version, pushes them to `ghcr.io/<owner>/<repo>` as `<version>` and `<version>-tex` (a stable release also moves `latest` and `tex`), and attests the build provenance of each by digest. It never writes over a tag that was built from another commit, and it stops on any registry answer other than a clear "no such manifest". It runs from its own `ghcr` environment and stays off until the repository variable `PUBLISH_IMAGES` is `true`. Never run: the workflow is tested against a stand-in `docker`, not a registry.
