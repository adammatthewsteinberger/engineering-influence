Screenplay scene cards may now carry a `**Tone:**` field, `warm`, `conflict` or `neutral`. When any card carries a tone, the scene-card stage refuses the cards unless the warm scenes are at least five times the conflict scenes (prompt 09's relational arc test), and it names any tone that is not one of the three. Cards with no tone are not counted, so a screenplay whose cards carry no tone is not checked. Compatible: the field is optional and no existing run changes.
