Seven publishing services no longer read files or PDFs themselves. A new `ManuscriptSourceInterface` (existence, text and Markdown listing) and a new `PdfInspectorInterface` (a `PdfDocument` of plain page facts, and a page count) sit between them and the file system, `pypdf` and `pdfplumber`; `FilesystemManuscriptSource` and `PypdfPdfInspector` are the adapters. `BookAuditor`, `ReleaseAuditor`, `PdfVerifier`, `PublishingController`, `PublishPipeline`, `PublishSteps` and `BookCli` now take those two collaborators as constructor arguments, and the PDF checks work on `PdfDocument` and `PdfPage` instead of library objects. The `ei` commands and their output are unchanged.
