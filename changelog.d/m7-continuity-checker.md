`StoryBible` is now a structured story bible (M7.1): characters with aliases and an age, places, dated events and a span in years, each short text one printable line and each list bounded. A new `ContinuityChecker` reads a novel's chapters against it, with no model call, and reports three kinds of finding: a capitalised name or place one letter from a known one (`Katharine` for `Katherine`), an age stated in one of three shapes that falls outside what the bible allows, and a dated event first mentioned after a later one. A finding is a question, not a verdict: a flashback is a good reason for a timeline finding to be wrong, a new character named like an old one for a spelling finding, and names under four letters are never compared. Nothing calls the checker yet; the novel stages that will are the next slices of M7.
