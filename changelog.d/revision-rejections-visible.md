An edit that a revision pass rejects is now reported as a tolerated `revision-rejected` issue, with the answer's word count and the length bounds it missed, and the chapter it was asked about is listed as kept as it was. Before, a rejected edit was reported as `revision-unchanged`, which read as the model having changed nothing. The rejection is recorded per chapter in `<stage>_rejected_NN.md`, so a redelivery reports the same. Nothing is kept or paid for differently; the run's status is unaffected.
