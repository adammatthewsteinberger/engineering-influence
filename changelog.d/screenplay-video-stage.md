A screenplay run gets a `video` stage after the EPUB. It plans the film's shots from the locked script (or the plain script before a lock): a scene noted `[[hero]]` and any scene longer than fifteen seconds go to Wan 3.0, a scene with several speeches goes to Gemini Omni Flash, and the rest go to MiniMax H3. Each shot is stored as `video/shot_NNN.mp4`, with `video/manifest.json` marking the film as AI-generated. The video is made only when `EI_VIDEO_PROVIDER=openrouter` and `EI_OPENROUTER_API_KEY` are set; otherwise the stage records that no video was made and spends nothing. Every shot not yet stored is priced first, and a run whose budget does not cover them waits for consent rather than spending past the cap. Compatible: a new optional stage, two environment settings and three error codes (`VIDEO_PROVIDER_UNAVAILABLE`, `VIDEO_GENERATION_FAILED`, `VIDEO_REJECTED`); existing screenplay runs gain a stage that makes no video unless a provider is configured.
