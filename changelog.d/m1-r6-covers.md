Moved the 35 cover classes out of `engineering_influence.publishing.covers` into the layers: `models/covers` (`Box`, `CoverLayout`, `CoverStyle`, `KdpCoverSpec`, and the layout and style rules `LayoutFactory`, `LayoutCheck`, `BuiltinCoverStyle`, `TextRecorder`), `infrastructure/covers` (the painter toolkit, typesetters, renderer, files, verifiers, `PainterRegistry`, `CoverPluginLoader` and `CoverBuilder`) and `interfaces` (the plugin contracts, now `CoverPainterInterface` and `CoverPluginInterface`). New `BookSlug` and `CoverBuilderInterface` keep the controller and the services from importing the cover adapters. `PublishingController` now asks a `CoverBuilderProvider` (built in `runtime/`) for its builder when covers are first needed, so cover plugins still load lazily.

**Deprecated, and removed in the next minor release:** the sixteen modules a private project imports from, `engineering_influence.publishing.covers.{box, box_blur, compositing, cover_builder, cover_layout, cover_painter, cover_plugin, cover_plugin_loader, cover_style, film_grain, ink, painter_registry, quiet_zone, soft_rect, starfield, value_noise}`. They still work and warn once on import. Import the same names from `engineering_influence.covers` instead (`CoverPainter` and `CoverPlugin` keep their names there); `ProjectLayout` is in the same place, replacing the already deprecated `engineering_influence.publishing.project_layout`. Both paths give you the same classes, so existing plugins and `isinstance` checks keep working. The other nineteen modules in that package were internal and are gone. The covers `ei book covers` and `ei book publish` write, and the `ei` commands and their output, are unchanged.
